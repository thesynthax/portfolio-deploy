Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This page explains what data is collected when you visit thesynthax.space, and what choices you have.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) via Google's gtag.js tag to understand how visitors use the site (e.g. pages viewed, general location at a country/city level, device type, and how long people explore the world). This helps improve the experience over time.

No account information, names, or payment details are collected — this is a personal portfolio with no sign-up or checkout flow.

Cookie consent

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you consent to analytics cookies. Your choice is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so you aren't asked again on the same device/browser.

  • Accept — Analytics cookies are set and your visit is included in Google Analytics reporting.
  • Reject — No analytics cookies are set. Google may still receive an anonymous, cookie-less signal used for aggregate modeling, but nothing tied to your browser is stored.

You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's site data for thesynthax.space (or clearing localStorage), which will show the banner again on your next visit.

What Google Analytics collects

Google Analytics uses cookies/identifiers to distinguish visitors and may collect IP address (used for approximate geolocation, then discarded), browser and device type, referring pages, and interactions with the site. Google's own handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms page.

Third parties

This site does not sell or share personal data with third parties other than Google, in its role as the analytics processor described above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the contact details listed on the portfolio itself.
